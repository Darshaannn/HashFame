# GGs Brand & Agency Onboarding Guide

## 1. Overview for Brands & Agencies

GGs is engineered to eliminate the manual chaos of finding, vetting, contacting, and contracting Indian content creators. Whether you are an in-house brand marketer or an agency talent director, GGs provides a structured collaboration workflow at zero software subscription cost.

## 3. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Register & Organization Setup
1. Sign in using your work email address via OTP.
2. Select your account type: **Brand** or **Agency**.
3. Enter your company name, website, and industry focus.

### Step 2: Intelligent Creator Search
Instead of scrolling endlessly or guessing usernames, use natural language queries:
- *"Fashion creators in Mumbai with over 50k followers"*
- *"Tech reviewers in Delhi NCR for gadget launch"*
- *"Fitness micro-influencers in Bangalore"*

The GGs search engine interprets tier keywords (Nano, Micro, Macro, Mega), city names, and category synonyms to rank relevant candidates instantly.

### Step 3: Review Creator Data Sources
When reviewing a profile, note the badges:
- **Verified Creator:** Registered user who actively receives in-app briefs and private quotation requests.
- **Instagram Connected:** Authenticated via official Meta Graph API; displays genuine engagement metrics and recent media grid.
- **Directory Profile:** Curated industry fixture. Contact requests will be delivered via their public business email until the creator registers.

### Step 4: Build Shortlists & Side-by-Side Comparison
1. Tap the **Bookmark / Shortlist** icon on any creator card.
2. Add to an existing campaign shortlist (e.g. *"Diwali Festive Campaign 2026"*) or create a new one.
3. Compare follower tiers, engagement rates, locations, and past deliverables in a unified view.

### Step 5: Send a Contact Request / Request a Private Quotation
1. On the creator's profile, tap **Contact Creator**.
2. Select purpose:
   - **Request Private Quotation:** When you have specific deliverables (e.g. 1 Instagram Reel + 2 Stories) and need their commercial rate.
   - **Direct Inquiry:** To open a direct conversation via their preferred communication channel (In-App Chat, WhatsApp, or Email).
3. Fill in campaign name, required deliverables, delivery date, and optional budget range.
4. Submit. The creator is notified immediately.
