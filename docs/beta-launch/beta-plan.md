# GGs Closed Beta Launch Plan

## 1. Objective & Scope

The primary objective of the GGs Closed Beta is to validate core marketplace liquidity, measure the usability of the search-to-outreach-to-quotation funnel, and identify product friction before general public release.

### Target Cohort Size
- **Creators:** 20–30 active creators
- **Brands & Agencies:** 5–10 active marketing managers, brand founders, or boutique agency talent leads
- **Target Inquiries:** 15–25 real collaboration inquiries initiated through the platform

---

## 2. Recommended Initial Market & Niche Selection

### Recommended Segment: **Fashion, Beauty & Lifestyle in Mumbai & Delhi NCR**

#### Rationale:
1. **Directory Density:** Over 55% of the initial 163-creator directory data is concentrated in Fashion, Beauty, Lifestyle, and Entertainment across Mumbai and Delhi NCR.
2. **High Deal Velocity:** Fashion and Lifestyle have the highest frequency of monthly influencer collaborations, gifting, and campaign cycles in India compared to long-cycle niches (e.g., Tech or B2B).
3. **Proximity & Liquidity:** Concentrating both sides of the marketplace in the Mumbai-Delhi corridor ensures that brands seeking regional creators find an immediate, relevant pool, maximizing early response rates.
4. **Private Quotation Fit:** Fashion/Beauty campaigns have diverse deliverable formats (Reel, Story set, Event appearance, Static post), perfectly demonstrating the power of GGs' private quotation model over rigid rate cards.

---

## 3. Phased Closed Beta Launch Timeline

```
Week 0: Pre-flight Verification (Stage 8 Clearance)
  │
  ├──► Week 1: Supply-Side Seeding (Creator Onboarding & Claims)
  │      └── Target: 25 active creators onboarded & profile completed
  │
  ├──► Week 2: Demand-Side Induction (Brand & Agency Cohort)
  │      └── Target: 8 brands/agencies onboarded; first search & shortlists
  │
  ├──► Week 3: Active Transactions & Outreach Funnel
  │      └── Target: Live contact requests, quotation submissions, and chat
  │
  └──► Week 4: Review, Scoring & Go/No-Go Decision
         └── Feedback synthesis, bug resolution, and launch review
```

### Phase Details

### Phase 1: Supply-Side Seeding (Days 1–7)
- Reach out to 40 pre-identified creators in Mumbai/Delhi (Fashion/Lifestyle).
- Onboard 20–25 creators into the Closed Beta APK (or internal distribution).
- Assist creators with claiming existing directory records or completing new profiles.
- Ensure contact preferences (email/WhatsApp) and category tags are accurately populated.
- Connect authorized Instagram accounts where test users are configured.

### Phase 2: Demand-Side Induction (Days 8–14)
- Introduce 5–10 boutique brands and agency talent managers via 1-on-1 personalized demos.
- Guide brands through natural language search, filtering, and shortlist creation.
- Ensure each brand creates at least 1 shortlist with 3–5 creators.

### Phase 3: Active Collaboration Testing (Days 15–21)
- Brands send genuine collaboration requests or quotation inquiries.
- Track creator response latency, acceptance rate, and quotation submission.
- Facilitate in-app communication and monitor deliverable submission flows.

### Phase 4: Feedback Synthesis & Evaluation (Days 22–28)
- Administer structured feedback questionnaires.
- Analyze completed collaboration funnels.
- Hold Stage 9 Review: Decide whether to **Expand Cohort**, **Iterate on Friction Points**, or **Hold for Feature Hardening**.
