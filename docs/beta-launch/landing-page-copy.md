# GGs Landing Page Specification & Copy

## 1. Design & Brand Language Guidelines

To maintain brand consistency with the GGs mobile application, all landing page components, hero sections, and buttons must adhere to the design system:
- **Primary Color:** Deep Burgundy (`#8B0000`)
- **Accent Color:** Dark Burgundy (`#680000`)
- **Backgrounds:** Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) with Light Gray surfaces (`#F8F9FA` / `#F0F2F5`)
- **Typography:**
  - **Headings & Badges:** `Khand` (Bold, uppercase tracking, impactful)
  - **Body Prose & Form Fields:** `Hind` (Regular, legible, warm Indian modern aesthetic)
- **Ethos:** Honest, professional, privacy-respecting. **No fake user counts, stock celebrity photos, or fabricated testimonial quotes.**

### [Section 6: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)]

**Q: Is GGs completely free to use?**  
A: Yes. During our Closed Beta phase, all features—including creator search, shortlisting, private quotation workflows, and campaign workspaces—are completely free for brands, agencies, and creators.

**Q: How do creators set their prices if there are no rate cards?**  
A: Every campaign is unique. When a brand sends an inquiry detailing their required deliverables (e.g., 1 Reel + 2 Stories), the creator replies with a private, customized quotation. This protects creator pricing and gives brands accurate quotes based on exact campaign scopes.

**Q: Does GGs post on my Instagram or ask for passwords?**  
A: Never. Instagram connection is handled entirely through Meta's official OAuth window. GGs never receives or stores your password and only requests read access to verify your follower count and media grid.

**Q: Who can see my business contact details?**  
A: Only logged-in, verified brands and agencies can see the contact options (Email, WhatsApp, or Phone) that you explicitly enable in your Contact Preferences. You can toggle them off at any time.
