# GGs Trust & Safety Policy and Operations

## 1. Principles of Marketplace Integrity

GGs is built on transparency, user consent, and accurate data representation. To protect all parties in the Indian creator ecosystem, GGs enforces clear policies regarding identity, account claims, communication conduct, and data accuracy.

---

## 2. Creator Identity Badges & Distinction

To avoid misleading brands or inflating creator credibility, GGs enforces four explicit tiers:

| Tier / State | What It Means | Verification Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directory Profile** | Curated reference profile imported from public industry indices. | Marked as "Directory Listing". Cannot receive in-app chats or submit quotations until claimed. |
| **Registered Creator** | Real user who signed up via verified Email OTP and completed profile. | Email ownership verified. Direct contact preferences active. |
| **Connected Instagram** | Creator authenticated via official Meta Graph API OAuth. | Token-backed live follower count and authorized media grid. |
| **Verified Identity** | Creator with verified government or corporate business registration. | Manual team review of GST / PAN / Agency representation contract. |

*Rule:* Never invent fake "Trust Badges" or automatic verification checkmarks without documented validation.

---

## 3. Account Claiming & Impersonation Prevention

### Claiming Process for Directory Profiles:
1. When a creator finds their pre-indexed directory profile, they submit a claim request.
2. **Automated Check:** If the registered email matches the published business email in the directory record, the claim is approved instantly.
3. **Manual Review:** If the email differs, the creator must:
   - Provide a verification DM from their official Instagram handle to the GGs Instagram account (`@ggs_app`), OR
   - Add a temporary verification code to their Instagram bio for 2 hours.
4. **Disputes:** If two parties claim the same profile, both accounts are frozen for manual review by the Trust & Safety team.

---

## 4. Anti-Spam & Contact Protection Policies

- **Rate Limiting:** Brands and agencies cannot dispatch more than 20 new contact requests per day to prevent automated spamming.
- **Clear Brief Requirement:** Contact requests must include a valid project title, deliverable scope, and target timeframe. Generic "Hey let's collab" messages without context are flagged for review.
- **Consent-Driven Visibility:** A creator's personal phone number or WhatsApp is never exposed unless the creator explicitly enables that specific channel in their Contact Preferences.

---

## 5. Disputed Quotations & Collaboration Disputes

- **Quotation Validity:** Quotations submitted through GGs represent private commercial proposals. Once accepted by the brand, the deliverable scope and payment schedule form a mutual agreement.
- **Reporting Mechanism:** Users can flag any collaboration, chat message, or user profile via the **Report** button.
- **Blocking:** Users can instantly block any brand, agency, or creator. Blocked users cannot send messages, view contact preferences, or submit quotation requests.

---

## 6. Manual Review Procedures

When an automated flag or user report is triggered:
1. **Triage:** Trust & Safety lead reviews the report within 24 hours.
2. **Investigation:** Inspect the reported chat log, contact request history, or profile images.
3. **Resolution:**
   - *False Alarm:* Dismiss report with notes.
   - *Minor Policy Breach (e.g. vague outreach):* Issue formal warning to user email.
   - *Severe Violation (e.g. harassment, impersonation, fake numbers):* Suspend account immediately.
