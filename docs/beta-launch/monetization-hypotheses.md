# GGs Monetization Validation & Pricing Hypotheses

## 1. Monetization Strategy Principles

- **Zero Fee During Closed Beta:** All features must remain 100% free for initial cohorts to remove all adoption friction and validate liquidity.
- **No Transaction Fees Without Escrow:** GGs will not charge take-rates on creator deals during Beta, as handling payouts without an RBI-compliant escrow or payment aggregator creates severe regulatory and operational overhead.
- **Value-Driven SaaS:** Revenue should originate from demand-side software value (agencies and high-volume brands), while keeping basic creator participation free.

---

## 2. Practical Monetization Models Evaluated for India

| Monetization Model | Description | Pros | Cons / Risks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Model A: Agency SaaS Subscription** *(Recommended)* | Monthly tier (e.g., ₹2,999 – ₹7,999/mo) for boutique agencies to manage unlimited client shortlists, export rosters, and track deliverables. | High willingness to pay; agencies already spend heavily on manual labor. Predictable recurring revenue. | Needs strong shortlist export and multi-user team features. |
| **Model B: Brand Campaign Pass** | Pay-per-campaign or quarterly brand pass (e.g. ₹1,499/campaign) for high-intent outreach credits. | Low barrier to entry for D2C brands doing seasonal campaigns. | Potential churn between campaign cycles. |
| **Model C: Transaction Commission (Take-rate)** | 5%–10% fee on contracted collaboration value. | Aligns platform with deal volume. | High risk of off-platform leakage (WhatsApp settlement). High compliance overhead (GST, TDS 194R/194C). |
| **Model D: Creator Premium (Pro Badge)** | ₹499/mo for creators for priority search ranking. | Direct monetization of supply side. | **High friction:** Creators resent paying platforms before closing guaranteed deals. Severely damages supply liquidity. |

## 3. Top Hypotheses to Validate During Beta Interviews

### Hypothesis 1: Boutique Agencies Will Pay for Shortlist & Quotation Workspaces
- **Premise:** Boutique digital and PR agencies spend 20+ hours per week manually searching Instagram, compiling client PPTs, and tracking rates in Excel.
- **Interview Test Question:**  
  *"If GGs allowed your team to build branded client shortlists, receive private binding quotes, and track deliverables for ₹3,499/month, would that save you enough hours to justify the cost?"*
- **Success Criteria:** At least 3 out of 5 interviewed agency directors state they would pay ₹3,000–₹5,000/mo.

### Hypothesis 2: Brands Value High-Fit Search Over Mass Scraping
- **Premise:** D2C brand founders are tired of paying ₹25,000+/mo for enterprise influencer platforms that only offer dead email addresses.
- **Interview Test Question:**  
  *"Would you prefer a lightweight tool that gives you 15 verified, responsive creator quotes for ₹1,999 per campaign rather than paying an expensive yearly subscription?"*
- **Success Criteria:** At least 4 out of 5 brand marketers confirm pay-per-campaign or micro-tier preference.

## 4. What NOT to Do Yet

- ❌ Do NOT gate essential search filters behind paywalls during the initial beta.
