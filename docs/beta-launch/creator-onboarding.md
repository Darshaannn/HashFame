# GGs Creator Onboarding Guide

## 1. Welcome to GGs

GGs is a creator collaboration platform built to connect you with verified brands and agencies for paid campaigns—without exposing your pricing or burying opportunities in spam DMs.

---

## 2. Creator Onboarding Checklist

Follow these steps to set up your profile for maximum discovery and high-value collaboration requests:

```
[ ] Step 1: Install & Register Account (Email OTP)
[ ] Step 2: Complete Creator Profile & Bio
[ ] Step 3: Set Primary & Secondary Niches / Categories
[ ] Step 4: Specify Base City & Location
[ ] Step 5: Configure Business Contact Channels & Privacy
[ ] Step 6: Connect Official Instagram Account (Optional / Authorized)
[ ] Step 7: (If applicable) Claim Existing Directory Record
[ ] Step 8: Review How Private Quotations Work
```

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Account Creation & Sign In
1. Open the GGs App and select **Sign In with Email**.
2. Enter your professional email address.
3. Check your inbox for the 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) sent via GGs.
4. Enter the OTP to authenticate. No passwords to remember or lose.

### Step 2: Complete Your Creator Profile
1. Navigate to the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation.
2. Enter your **Display Name** (how brands know you) and **Handle**.
3. Write a concise bio (150–300 characters) describing your content style, audience focus, and key themes.

### Step 3: Tag Your Niches & City
1. Select up to 3 relevant categories (e.g., `Fashion`, `Beauty`, `Lifestyle`). This powers the GGs intelligent search algorithm so relevant brands find you easily.
2. Set your primary city (e.g., `Mumbai`, `New Delhi`, `Bengaluru`).

### Step 4: Configure Contact Preferences (Privacy First)
GGs gives you granular control over how brands can contact you:
1. Open **Contact Preferences** in Settings or Profile.
2. Toggle **Accept Collaboration Requests** to ON.
3. Choose whether to expose your **Business Email**, **WhatsApp Number**, or **Phone Number**.
   > *Note: These direct contact details are never shown publicly on the web. They are only visible to authenticated brands and agencies inside GGs when enabled.*

### Step 5: Connect Your Instagram Account
If enabled for your beta cohort:
1. Tap **Connect Instagram** in your profile editor.
2. Authorize via Meta's official login window.
3. GGs will verify your handle and display your recent media grid and authorized follower count.
   > *GGs never asks for your Instagram password and cannot post on your behalf.*

## 4. Understanding Directory Listings vs. Registered Accounts

| Feature | Directory Listing (Unclaimed) | Registered & Claimed Creator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source** | Public industry reference index. | Verified account owner. |
| **In-App Messaging** | Disabled until claimed. | Fully active in real-time. |
| **Quotation Requests** | Not possible. | Direct, customized quotation flow. |
| **Direct Contact** | Shows publicly listed business email (if any). | Shows creator's opted-in preferences. |
| **Badge** | Marked as "Directory Profile". | Marked as "Verified Creator Profile". |

### How to Claim Your Directory Profile
If your name or handle is already in the GGs Directory:
1. Find your profile via Search.
2. Tap **"Is this you? Claim this profile"**.
3. Verify ownership via email matching the profile or submit a 1-click verification request.
4. All past directory saves and shortlists will immediately link to your registered account.

## 5. How Private Quotations Work (No Public Rate Cards)

At GGs, we do not believe in public rate cards. Your pricing depends on usage rights, content complexity, and timeline:
1. When a brand wants to work with you, they send a **Contact Request** outlining their deliverables, timeline, and estimated budget.
2. You receive an alert in your **Incoming Requests**.
3. Tap **Accept & Submit Quotation**.
4. Enter your proposed fee (in ₹ INR), select delivery days, and outline any specific terms.
5. The brand receives your private proposal and can accept or chat directly in GGs.
